Support live
Support requests can be raised any time through the public Jira Service Management portal or by email at support@abuzararif.xyz.

01Hours of operation

Support is monitored Monday through Friday, 09:00–18:00 Pakistan Standard Time (UTC+5), excluding local public holidays. Requests submitted outside these hours are picked up the next business day.

03Support channels

Severity is assessed on first response based on impact described in the request. Paid-tier customers and issues affecting attachment uploads or data integrity are prioritized first.
